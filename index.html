Every tool is free to open, needs no login, and keeps student work on the student's own device.

About the work

How the tools are built

  • I design each tool, write the competencies, and check all of the science and every answer key.
  • AI tools write and test the software from my specifications.
  • I pilot each tool in my own courses and revise it from what students do.

Teaching and service

  • Full-time Professor of Biology, Solano Community College, teaching anatomy, physiology, and microbiology.
  • Curriculum Committee and Distance Education Committee, American River College.
  • Founder, MedMasters Collaborative, LLC, which trains faculty to design active learning with AI.

Military medical background

  • U.S. Air Force veteran, with service as an Aerospace Medical Technician and in medical training and instruction.
